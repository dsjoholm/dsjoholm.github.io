Please check out some various projects of mine below:

[Extreme Heat and Community Resilience Evaluation Project](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1TeFDEzoELQkyUsLml4YWCxpjPq8kEEPW/view?usp=sharing): My graduate thesis at Sciences Po Paris, completed in a group of 6. Summarizes our travels and interviews in Europe, and makes recommendations for decision-makers in California. Tackles issues of high heat in terms of systems planning, architectural regulations, governmental prioritization, and transportation.

[Pathways to Perseverance](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1H1NM52nMWLzavGJf20MrzRbr  -hxBNSmb/view): My undergraduate thesis at UC San Diego. Compares coastal railway systems in California and the Netherlands, gives suggestions on improving climate resilience of coastal transportation networks.

[Burdens for Affordable Housing Residents in San Diego](https://ucits.org/projects/examining-transportation-access-and-cost-burdens-for-affordable-housing-residents-in-san-diego/): A Homelessness Hub project. Using quantitative and qualitative methods, we determined the transportation burden—both monetary and temporal—for people living in 6 low-income housing developments in San Diego.

[A Politically Viable Road User Charge in San Diego County](https://drive.google.com/file/d/14DsNos1jy2DNBuTS9WtHRtcd7dE6v8qw/view): A UCLA class project. Conducted extensive research to put together a 3-page policy brief about road user charging in Southern California. 

[Climate Change Gazette](https://www.canva.com/design/DAFij0JuUs0/W1sYDBEzWQERcNOnr6HNmQ/edit?utm_content=DAFij0JuUs0&utm_campaign=designshare&utm_medium=link2&utm_source=sharebutton): A UCSD internship project. With a partner, created a 10-page interactive and engaging newspaper about various facets of the climate change issue. 

[Investigation of car crashes in school zones](https://colab.research.google.com/drive/1VJ8nAs1jAjws0kwCS6RiutKDoaFBaq_e?usp=sharing): A Sciences Po project. Gathered, cleaned, and analyzed geospatial data in Python to determine trends in car crashes near schools, and their correlation with environmental quality.

[Activation and greening of Porte de La Villette, Paris](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1CVO9yvfSt-SyHgzqHs3ghM6x1Mmj62vr/view?usp=sharing): A Sciences Po project. Worked in a group of 3 to devise environmental, economic, and and safety-related improvements to Paris' Porte de la Villette area.
