## Recently watched

- Farewell My Concubine (1993)
- Le lion volatil (2003) - A surrealist, almost completely nonsensical and charming small film about a disappearing man. Fun cat special effects at the end. Worth watching for its charm and wit, some stilted acting.
- Daguerréotypes (1975) - Slowww and mesmerizing look into a hyperlocal stretch of rue Daguerre in the mid 1970s. What struck me was the hyperspecific businesses, like clock repairmen and typewriters, that have all been replaced by computers. Terrific French accents with rolled r's. Amazing fashion; everyone here would be the best dressed person in any 2026 room. Surprising to see a huge number of husband-wife duos in shops. Almost everyone was born elsewhere and has "monté" to Paris, showing the rapidity of France's urbanization. Love the theme of the magician, Mystag, who was [also an anarchist activist](https://maitron.fr/francois-robert-dit-mystag-dictionnaire-des-anarchistes/). Amazing cuts between related scenes and ideas; in this way, oddly enough, it reminds me of Jet Lag The Game.
