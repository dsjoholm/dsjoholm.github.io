Welcome!

Thank you for finding this website. I'm glad you're here. My name is Daniel: I'm an American urban planning student in Paris. I do hope to be writing here frequently, and I've also gathered my projects and professional experience.

The writings here should be a good look into my personality and what I enjoy. This being an entirely amateur production, there's no comments, no mailing list, and no other fluff. I want this to make my website somewhere that I can write not only without distractions, but without (too much) feedback as well.

I don't as of yet know what I'll write about, if knowing that is even possible! I aim to be relatively frequent in publishing, but that is being left vague extremely intentionally.
