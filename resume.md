# Professional Experience

### Transportation Planning Intern, CDM Smith

Bellevue, WA, USA (June 2025 - August 2025)

In this role, my first one at a private consulting company, I worked in CDM Smith's innovative **road user charging team** and participated in multiple projects. To aid a redesign of Chicago's I-290 Corridor, I wrote **policy and funding recommendations** for Illinois’ Blue Ribbon Commission on Transportation Infrastructure Funding and Policy. I helped implement and analyze data in support of new tolling technology in Washington State, and performed **subsidy analyses for road user charge studies** in Washington and Nevada.

### Student Transportation and Housing Researcher, UC San Diego Homelessness Hub

San Diego, CA, USA (October 2022 - September 2024)

As a student researcher, I was actively involved in both academic- and community-focused projects. Primarily, I developed and studied ways to improve the **equitability and sustainability** of San Diego’s transportation systems. I also served as the Homelessness Hub's data manager and created **geospatial datasets** on homelessness in San Diego along with mapping and analyzing crime and other social phemonena. Furthermore, I had the opportunity to liaison with government agencies like SANDAG and the City of San Diego.  

### Fire Risk and Resilience Activist, San Diego Supercomputer Center

San Diego, CA, USA (August 2022 - April 2024)

I had two main roles in this position. First of all, I used the San Diego Supercomputer Center's resources to monitor and model, in real time, fires emerging throughout the state and communicate information about their potential **risk and danger** to firefighters on the ground. I also used proprietary climate and transportation data in order to **analyze the risk climate change poses** to California’s roads and rail rights-of-way.

### Airport Transit and Planning Intern, San Diego Airport

San Diego, CA, USA (June 2023 - August 2023)

My main charge as an airport transit intern was to propose a **wholesale redesign of a bus route** connecting the airport to a nearby rail station. To do this, I authored and deployed a comprehensive rider survey studying the quality public bus service to the airport and used transportation modeling software to recommend a new bus route, improved frequency, and better wayfinding. These proposals were later put into practice by the airport.
